CAMPUS CONNECT
Multilingual Assistant

A language-agnostic AI chatbot designed to bridge the communication gap between students and college administration. Unlike basic FAQ bots, Campus Connect processes **PDF circulars** and answers queries in regional languages, eliminating long queues for routine information like fees and scholarships.

Innovation Highlights

  • Multilingual Support: Understands & replies in local regional languages.
  • Document Intelligence: Reads official PDF circulars automatically.
  • Zero-Queue System: Instant answers for fees, exam schedules, and notices.
  • Open Source: Low-cost architecture for easy college adoption.
